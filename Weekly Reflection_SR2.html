P 9
Personal Declaration
Reflection  ·  Sep 2026

The first San Ramon felt like a beginning. This one felt like a landmark.

Coming back to the same campus with months of real accounts, real customer conversations, and genuinely difficult moments behind me made every session land differently. What SR1 introduced as theory, SR2 confirmed as experience. The frameworks did not change. The judgment behind applying them did.

"The Capstone brought the whole journey into focus. Every coaching session, every assignment, every week of field work was the preparation. Building it revealed as much as delivering it — how to take something complex and make it clear, how to tell the story of value in a way that actually lands."

Transitioning from the foundations of SR1 through four months of real customer work, and finally delivering the Capstone in SR2, made the entire Academy journey come full circle. Stepping back into the field with stronger skills, a broader perspective, and real confidence as a Customer Success Manager.

What stays most is the network — not just the contacts, but the shared experience. People across APAC, EMEA, and beyond who have gone through exactly the same journey. A global network built not from exchanging business cards but from going through the whole thing together. Having a global network of friends I can message anytime is what I will treasure most.
